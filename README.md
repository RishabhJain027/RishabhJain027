### 👋 About Me

- 🎓 Engineering student at **SAKEC** (Shah & Anchor Kutchhi Engineering College), Mumbai
- 🔌 Focused on **IoT and embedded hardware**
- 🎥 Cinematographer & creative technologist — I shoot, edit, and build reels alongside code
- 🧠 Currently building **[Rishabh Engine](https://github.com/RishabhJain027)** — a cinematic, interactive 3D portfolio (React Three Fiber, GSAP, Zustand, custom GLSL shaders)

- 💬 Ask me about embedded systems, structural health monitoring, or how to light a shot
